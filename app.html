It is one continuous 3D world you fly through, not a scrolling page and not a video. No video is embedded anywhere — the reference footage was used only as a visual target.

What's in the build

  • One scroll-sampled camera flight. Eleven narrative stations (hero → node → traffic → code → six scenarios → finale) are laid into a single 3D world: the globe at the origin, the six test chambers arranged on a ring around it. Scroll sets one progress value; the camera damps toward the sampled path point, so fast scrolling reads as weight, not a jump cut, and scrolling back up reverses the flight.
  • Continuous animation independent of scroll. A single shared clock advances on useFrame; globe rotation, hub ring spin, hub cores, packet flow, attack streams, towers, orbits, city lights, acoustic rings and marine snow all keep running when you stop. Camera "breathing" keeps the frame alive too.
  • Scene 1-2: glass fresnel topology globe with a geodesic lattice and 20 metallic arcs, 14 surface hubs + two large mechanical hubs (disc, counter-rotating rings, spokes, pulsing emissive core) — the shell dissolves as you travel inside.
  • Scene 3: Point-to-Point link built as a braided cable (6 helical strands + glowing core + lit collars) with 26 packets in both directions, each carrying an 8-point additive trail.
  • Scene 4: C++ terminal with a real typewriter, token highlighting and a live simulator log, beside a Wireshark-style capture (filter bar, throughput strip, live rows, hex/ASCII detail). The 3D keeps running behind it, defocused via a scroll-staged blur stage.
  • Scenes 5-10: cybersecurity (shield with impact flash, red attack streams, blue defence waves), 5G/6G (moving vehicle, 5 towers, expanding cell waves, visible handover beam), V2X (roads, instanced traffic, V2V links, cycling signals), satellites (night-side Earth with 900 city lights, atmosphere shell, 3 orbiting sats, beams and latency-varied data paths), IoT city (120 buildings, 240 sensors, 160 rising traffic streams, scanning ring), underwater (volumetric caustics shader, patrolling submarine with spinning propeller and lamp cone, 3 drones, propagating acoustic rings, 1 500 drifting motes).
  • Finale: six streams of motes converge back into the globe while it relights, over the docs/Q&A block.

GLSL shaders handle the shell fresnel/lattice, the water caustics volume, and all point/sprite glow. Instancing covers buildings, windows, sensors, vehicles, collars, stars and dust; mobile drops particle and instance counts to ~42%, lowers DPR and disables post-processing. prefers-reduced-motion freezes the autonomous animation and disables the blur stage. Each subsystem (the WebGL island, the post pass) sits behind its own error boundary so nothing can blank the page.

Fixed and live: ns3-network-engine

What was actually wrong. The six chamber cameras were authored on the ring radius with no outward stand-off, so instead of sitting back from each scene they sat ~18 units above its centre. That is why every scene was cropped.

The fix (framing only). Each chamber station now stores its authored view direction (outward from the globe, with a slight lift) plus the measured half-extents of its important content. Every frame the camera resolves its distance along that ray as the larger of two constraints:

  • vertical: contentHeight / tan(fov/2)
  • horizontal: contentWidth / (tan(fov/2) × viewportAspect)

plus a 15% surround margin. The horizontal constraint relaxes on a wide screen and dominates on a portrait phone, which pulls the camera back exactly as far as the composition needs. Because it re-resolves per frame, rotating a device or resizing the window reshapes the framing live, and the existing camera damping turns that into a smooth move rather than a jump.

Two supporting adjustments, both required by the pull-back and in scope: the IoT view aims 18 units higher so the 59-unit central tower is framed instead of forcing a 1.5× further retreat, and the fog near plane moved 70 → 130 (far 820 → 1900) so a chamber pulled back for a narrow viewport stays crisp instead of dissolving into the void.
